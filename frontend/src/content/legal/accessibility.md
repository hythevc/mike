{{legalEntityName}} wants {{productName}} to be usable by as many people as possible, including people who use assistive technology.

## Our target

{{productName}} aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. The interface is built to keep a visible focus indicator in every theme, to give interactive controls accessible names, to respect a reduced-motion preference, and to keep text and controls legible at a readable contrast.

## Known gaps

Accessibility is ongoing work. Some third-party content and some documents you upload (for example, scanned PDFs) may not meet the same standard, and {{productName}} flags pages it cannot read rather than hiding them.

## Giving feedback

If you meet a barrier using {{productName}}, tell us at {{supportEmail}} and describe the page and what went wrong. {{legalEntityName}} will work with you to provide the information or function another way while it fixes the problem.
