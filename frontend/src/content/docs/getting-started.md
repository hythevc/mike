This page takes you from a new account to a first reviewed document.

## Create an account

Sign up with an email address and password, or with a Google account. Where the operator has enabled single sign-on, the sign-in page also offers it for the email domains it covers. After signing up you complete a short setup: your name, your practice details, any invitations waiting for you, and the model step below. You also accept the [Terms of Service]({{termsUrl}}) and the [Privacy Notice]({{privacyUrl}}) at their current versions.

## Connect a model

{{productName}} runs on a model provider of your choice. In the last setup step you can save your own provider key, which is encrypted before it is stored and used only for the calls you make. Where the operator has configured a key for the deployment, you can start with that instead and add your own later. A usable model is selected by default, so the assistant is ready as soon as setup ends.

## Open the sample matter

The assistant's start screen offers a short first-run guide. Its first step opens a sample matter built from public contract templates, so you can try everything on documents that are not client data. The matter is named so that nobody mistakes it for a client file.

## Ask a question

Type a question about a document in the assistant. The answer quotes the passages it relied on, and each quote is matched against the document's text and labelled with the result: found, partly found, or not found. A quote that was not found is marked for you to check against the source. You can ask the assistant to answer only from the documents in the conversation; if they do not hold the answer, it says so instead of guessing.

## Review documents and run workflows

Workflows apply a saved set of instructions to a document and produce a draft. Tabular reviews run the same questions across many documents and show the results as a table, with a quote status on every cell (see [Tabular review and quote status](/docs/tabular-review-and-quote-status)). A playbook review compares a contract against your standard positions and lists each finding with the clause it matched, its reasons and suggested language; you accept, override with a note, or escalate each finding, and every decision is recorded with your name and the time.

## Review tracked changes

Edits the assistant proposes arrive as tracked changes. Nothing is applied until you accept it, and you can reject any change. In the browser you review them in the document view; in Microsoft Word you review them in the document itself.

## Invite colleagues

An organization groups the people of a firm or a legal team. Owners and administrators invite members, set roles and see the organization's audit log. Invitations appear in the app when the invited person opens it.

## Work from Microsoft Word

The task-pane add-in brings the same assistant into Word. Sign in from the pane, ask about the open document, and receive proposed edits as tracked changes in the document you are working on.
