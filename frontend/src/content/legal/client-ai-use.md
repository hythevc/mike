This note explains, in plain language, how this firm uses AI tools such as {{productName}} when working on your matter. A firm can give this note to a client, and adapt the engagement-letter clause at the end.

## How we use AI on your matter

We use AI software to help us review, draft and compare documents more quickly. The software suggests text, highlights passages and drafts answers. A lawyer at the firm reads and decides on everything before it is used or sent. The AI does not make decisions on your matter and does not replace your lawyer's judgement.

## What this means for your information

To do this work, the relevant documents are processed by the software and the model providers it uses. Those providers process the text in a stated region and do not use it to train their models. We keep your documents and the resulting drafts in a secure system, and we delete them in line with our records policy and your instructions.

## Your choices

You can ask us not to use AI tools on all or part of your matter, and we will record and follow that choice. Tell your lawyer if you have questions or restrictions, for example because of a third party's confidentiality requirements.

## Confidentiality and responsibility

Using these tools does not change our duty of confidentiality or our professional responsibilities to you. We remain responsible for the work, including anything the software helped produce.

## Engagement letter clause

A firm may adapt the following clause for its engagement letter:

> Use of AI tools. We may use artificial-intelligence software to assist with reviewing, drafting and comparing documents on your matter. A lawyer reviews and remains responsible for all work product. Your information is processed only as described in our privacy materials and is not used to train AI models. You may ask us in writing to limit or stop the use of these tools on your matter, and we will follow that request.
