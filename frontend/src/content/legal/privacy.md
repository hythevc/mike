This Privacy Notice explains how {{legalEntityName}} handles personal data when you use {{productName}}. For personal data you upload about your own clients and matters, you are the controller and {{legalEntityName}} is your processor under the Data Processing Agreement.

## What we collect

- Account data: your name, email, organisation and authentication details.
- Content you provide: the documents and instructions you upload for analysis.
- Usage data: records needed to run the service, bill it where enabled, and keep it secure.

## How we use it

{{legalEntityName}} uses personal data to provide and secure the service, to support you, and to meet legal obligations. Your uploaded content is processed to produce the drafts, answers and findings you ask for. {{legalEntityName}} does not use your content to train models.

## Sharing and sub-processors

{{legalEntityName}} shares personal data only with the sub-processors listed in the Subprocessors notice, which includes the model providers that run inference, and only as needed to provide the service. Each is bound by appropriate data protection terms.

## International transfers

Where personal data moves across borders, {{legalEntityName}} relies on an appropriate transfer mechanism, such as the applicable standard contractual clauses.

## Retention and deletion

{{legalEntityName}} keeps account data for the life of the account and content for as long as you keep it in the service. You can delete your content at any time. After you delete an item or close your account, {{legalEntityName}} removes the data from active systems within 30 days, except where the law requires it to be kept.
