# Negotiation playbook

Standard positions for a commercial contract review.

## Limitation of liability

- Preferred: a mutual cap at the fees paid in the prior twelve months.
- Fallback: a cap at two times the fees paid.
- Walk-away: any uncapped indirect liability.

## Indemnities

- Preferred: indemnity limited to third-party IP claims.
- Fallback: add a data-breach indemnity with a separate, higher cap.

## Term and termination

- Preferred: termination for convenience on sixty days' notice.
- Fallback: termination for convenience after an initial one-year term.
